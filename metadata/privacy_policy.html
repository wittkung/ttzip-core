TTZip Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

Your privacy is our utmost priority. TTZip is designed from the ground up as a native, local-first utility for macOS.

1. Zero Data Collection

TTZip does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, hardware fingerprints, file contents, or usage analytics.

  • No Network Tracking: The application does not contain any third-party tracking SDKs or behavioral telemetry.
  • Local-First Operations: All archive compression, decompression, hashing, and preview rendering occur entirely on your local machine.
  • Zero Cloud Upload: Your files never leave your device.

2. Cryptographic Security & Passwords

When you create or open password-protected archives (AES-256):

  • Passwords reside only in transient, memory-locked RAM.
  • Sensitive memory is actively zeroized (wiped) immediately after the operation finishes to prevent memory scraping.

3. Updates & Verification

  • Mac App Store / Steam: Software updates and payment processing are handled exclusively by Apple and Valve respectively in accordance with their privacy guidelines.
  • Direct / Community Edition: License verification is evaluated 100% offline using Ed25519 public-key cryptography with zero network requests.
